Fiable is a subscription-based last-mile delivery service for Lagos businesses, vendors and online sellers.

Choose a plan, pay upfront, receive your monthly delivery units and book deliveries through the Fiable process.

Basic includes 45 units, Growth includes 75 units and Business includes 110 units. A delivery can use between 1 and 7 units based on its route.

Unused units may roll over for no more than two weeks.
