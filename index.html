Questions, answered

Before you book.

Apexwings is a subscription-based last-mile delivery service for Lagos businesses, vendors and online sellers.

Choose a plan, pay upfront, receive your monthly delivery units and book deliveries through the Apexwings process.

Basic includes 45 units, Growth includes 75 units and Business includes 110 units. A delivery can use between 1 and 7 units based on its route.

We operate across structured zones in Mainland, Island, Lekki and selected extended areas including Ikorodu, Badagry, Epe and Apapa.

Subscribers can request three or more deliveries from one pickup address across supported locations. Destinations are grouped according to your pickup and delivery areas. Each group of 3–5 deliveries qualifies for 5%, 6–10 for 10%, and 11+ for 15% off route units. Groups of one or two use normal units. Charges round up to whole units with at least one unit per delivery, so some batches may have no unit saving. Review the result in your portal before confirming.

Unused units may roll over for no more than two weeks.

Urgent and food delivery requirements may be handled according to operational availability. Express delivery is ₦2,000 per unit.

Complete the application on our Get Started page. A Apexwings representative will contact you to confirm your business needs and next steps.
