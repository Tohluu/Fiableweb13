How the estimate works We compare the pickup and delivery locations, assign their operational zones, then estimate units based on the route. Same-location routes use 1 unit, nearby neighborhoods such as Yaba and Surulere use about 2 units, moderate cross-city routes use 3 units, and Lekki or extended routes use more.
